Growth rate and capacity

Keep capacity fixed and change the growth rate. Predict whether the ceiling moves.

Change and compare

growth rate r, range 0.1..2 per time unit
carrying capacity K, range 100..2000 individuals
N0 (initial population, fixed) = 10 individuals
N0 is fixed for this experiment; only r and K vary.

Read the result

t = 0.00 time units N = 10.0 individuals dN/dt = 0.0 individuals/time K = 1000 individuals r = 0.50 /time

Model and assumptions

dN/dt = rN(1 − N/K)

N(t) = K / (1 + Ae^(−rt))
A = (K − 10)/10

Initial population: 10 individuals. Time: 0–20 units. N and K: individuals; r: 1/time. The dashed line marks K.

Closed population; constant r and K; continuous growth without delay or randomness. Growth is fastest at N = K/2. Changing r alters the approach to K; changing K moves the ceiling.
